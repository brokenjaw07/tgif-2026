Registration (6:30PM)

Setup2 round registration tables just inside the door along the left wall, plus the reception table on the right. Each round table is a full counter: PayNow QR on a stand, a cash box with small notes for change, wristbands, and the paid-online list. Reception is the help desk and opens as a 3rd counter if the queue gets long.
Outside the doorThe queue splits into 2 lines, one for each counter. A crew member at the door keeps both lines moving and tells people to get their PayNow ready while they wait.
At the counterPaid online: tick the name off the list. PayNow: check the success screen (amount + name). Cash: take it and give change. Then crew puts the wristband on the person's wrist so it can't be passed on.
Idea: 2 wristband coloursOne colour for church youths, another for friends they brought. Leaders can spot new friends at a glance and make sure no one sits alone.
When the queue dies down (~7:15)Clear the 2 round tables (they can become extra seats with chairs for people who don't want to sit on the floor). Reception handles late arrivals until about 8:30. Never leave a cash box alone.
After the eventCount cash and PayNow for each counter, and check the total matches the number of wristbands used.

Food line (4 chapel tables)

1. DIY tacos / burritos + nachosSame fillings and toppings (mince, cheese, salsa, sour cream), so they share one table.
2. DIY potato spudsRight after the tacos so the leftover toppings (cheese, sour cream) can be passed along or shared.
3. Pasta + finger foodThe hot food. Needs warmers, so check where the power points are.
4. Churros + melted chocolateDessert last. The logs list has chocolate compound, so it gets melted, which needs a microwave or warmer. Keep the drips away from the savoury food.
Canned drinks + Jarritos: in the pantry fridgeNo ice. Clear the fridge before setup so the drinks, sour cream, cheese and juices all fit. Put the overflow cans in early so they're cold by 6:30.
Drinks: big wooden table by the fridgeDIY mocktails + face-straw printer, away from the food queue.
